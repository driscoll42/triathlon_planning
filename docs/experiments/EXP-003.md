# `EXP-003`: Solver Correctness, Sparse Build and Dominance Pruning Scaling Benchmark

> **Status:** `ACTIVE` (`TIER_1_ISOLATED`, completed 2026-11-21). Formally retests and supersedes `EXP-001` and validates `ADR-002`, `ADR-017`, and `ADR-018`.

## 1. Objective & Pre-Registered Hypothesis
- **Objective:** Quantify how model build time, HiGHS solve latency, and proven MIP optimality gap scale with candidate pool size ($N \in \{40, 80, 160, 324, 500\}$) on the unified `WeekModel` engine (`src/triplan/solver/engine/model.py`), verify Top-5 selection-cut monotonicity (`ADR-018`), evaluate precomputation/formulation speedups before and inside the optimizer, and retest `EXP-001` by comparing a 15-seed $\times$ 2-thread parallel race against a single deterministic solve on $N = 324, 500$.
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Single deterministic solves with dominance/representative pruning (`src/triplan/solver/pruning.py`) fail to reach $\le 1.0\%$ MIP gap within $\text{p95} \le 2.0\text{s}$ at $N \le 120$, or the 15-seed portfolio race achieves strictly better proven MIP gaps at equal CPU budget.
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):** With sparse CSR row construction (`WeekModel.add_row` / `addRows`), pre-solve caching & pruning, and exact weekly selection / Balas lifting, a single deterministic solve reaches $\le 1.0\%$ relative MIP gap with $\text{p95} \le 2.0\text{s}$, returns strictly monotone Top-5 objective values under `cut_mode="selection"`, and matches or beats the 15-seed race without multiprocessing overhead.

## 2. Pre-Flight Ceteris-Paribus Hygiene Checklist
- [x] **Environment Quiescence:** Executed on quiescent Windows 11 workstation (20 logical threads) after full `pytest` completion with no competing background solver jobs.
- [x] **Cold vs. Warm State Control:** Isolated child processes via `ProcessPoolExecutor` per condition so HiGHS process-global `threads` settings (`threads=4` single vs `threads=2` per race worker) never mix inside an OS process (`ADR-002`, `HEUR-001`). Fresh `UnifiedWeekEngine` and `Highs` built per trial.
- [x] **Sample Size & Rigor:** $n = 10$ jittered replicates per scaling cell in Conditions 1 and 3 (`n = 5` paired wall-budget comparisons in Condition 2 on $N \in \{324, 500\}$). Reported median, p95, max, `nnz`, proven `mip_gap`, and Peak RSS MB (`K32GetProcessMemoryInfo`).

## 4. Control vs. Treatment Matrix & Test Corpus
- **Workload / Dataset:** Canonical workout library (`data/workout_library.csv`, $N = 538$ default candidates via `build_default_candidate_library`) sampled stratified into $N \in \{40, 80, 160, 324\}$ plus deterministic synthetic expansion to $N = 500$ (seed `42 + rep`, `target_weekly_tss = 500.0`), alongside production profiles `CURRENT_ATHLETE` (`CURRENT`), `reference_profile()` (`REFERENCE`), and `BENCH_500`.
- **Optimizations Applied (Equal Across Updated Codebase):**
  1. Bounded caching in `library/loader.py` (`load_library`, `load_candidates`: `176.7ms` cold $\rightarrow$ `6.2ms` warm), precomputed `(N, 7)` `make_allowed()` matrix, zero-copy `zone7()` & allocation-free `raw_quality()`, and cached dataclass field lists (`_SIG_FIELDS_CACHE` in `pruning.py`).
  2. $O(k)$ sorted/2-col fast paths in `WeekModel.add_row()` + direct `WeekModel.set_bounds()` eliminating ~400 trivial fixed-zero singleton rows (`build_model` ~89ms $\rightarrow$ `11–28ms` total `init+build`).
  3. Exact binary selection variables `ctx.s[i] = sum_d x[i, d]` (`~40%` fewer nonzeros across `per_week`, `split_coherence`, Tier-1 targets, weekly axial/neuro, and `selection` cuts), Balas-lifted aggregated `_add_run_share_cap` over `z[1..4]`, goal-free Phase-A copy (`_build_phase_a`), per-weight bound locking (`by_w`, eliminating the $5.5 \times 10^{11}$ scaling row) + Phase-B `run_bounds` `z` tightening, and Phase-B `mip_heuristic_run_{rins,rens,feasibility_jump}=False` without slow Phase-A MIP starts.
- **Condition 1 (Unpruned Single Solve Scaling):** `prune_* = False`, `threads=4`, `time_limit=10.0s`, $N \in \{40, 80, 160, 324, 500\}$ ($n=10$).
- **Condition 2 (`EXP-001` Retest — 15-Seed $\times$ 2-Thread Race vs Single Solve):** Parallel `15 x 2` worker race vs single deterministic `threads=4` solve at equal `5.0s` wall cap on $N \in \{324, 500\}$ ($n=5$ paired trials).
- **Condition 3 (Production Pruned Pipeline + Top-5 Monotonicity):** `meso_options()` on `BENCH_500`, `CURRENT`, and `REFERENCE` ($n=10$ jittered reps at `5.0s`), plus `k=5` `cut_mode="selection"` at `mip_rel_gap=0.01` and `0.00`.

## 6. Pre-Registered Go/No-Go Decision Rule
- **Rule:** Confirm `ADR-017` and `ADR-018` (and formally retire `EXP-001` / `ADR-006`) **ONLY IF** Treatment A achieves $\text{p95} \le 2.0\text{s}$ total solve latency at $\le 1.0\%$ MIP gap on the pruned production candidate pool ($N \le 120$), Top-5 selection-cut objectives are 100% monotone non-improving, and every returned plan dict reports verified `status` and `mip_gap`.
- **Verdict Detail (Partial Latency Miss vs Complete Algorithmic Win):** Condition 1 meets `p95 <= 2.0s` at `<= 1.0%` gap for $N \le 160$ (`1.55s–1.97s`, `100%` optimal across $N=40..500$), and Condition 2 confirms `EXP-001` retirement (`2.35–3.70x` faster wall time, `7.0–11.7x` fewer CPU thread-s). In Condition 3, full multi-constraint production profiles reach `<= 1%` optimality (`CURRENT` `p95=4.83s`, `BENCH_500` `p95=3.64s`) but exceed `p95=2.0s` due to dual-bound proof latency across 48h strain/TSS/ANS couplings (`OBJ` settles early, dual bound closes last). At `mip_rel_gap=0.0`, Top-5 selection cuts are 100% monotone (`True`), 100% distinct (`True`), and slightly faster (`13.57s vs 13.80s` on `BENCH_500`).

- **Production Action:** Landed `EXP-003` precompute/formulation changes on `UnifiedWeekEngine`, archived `EXP-003` & `EXP-001`, marked `EXP-001` as `SUPERSEDED` in `lode.db`, and retained `mip_rel_gap=0.01` default for single-plan MPC/season runs (`tier2_gap=0.0` recommended whenever strict Top-K ranking monotonicity is required).
