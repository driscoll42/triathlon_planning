# `EXP-001`: Multi-Seed HiGHS Race vs Single-Seed Branching Search Benchmark

> **SUPERSEDED by `EXP-003` (2026-11-21) & `ADR-018`:** Retested in Condition 2 of `EXP-003` (`scripts/run_exp003_benchmark.py`) across `N = 324` and `N = 500`. A single deterministic `UnifiedWeekEngine` solve (`threads=4`) matched the `15-seed x 2-thread` race median objective identically (`-48.8045` on `N=324`, `-56.1340` on `N=500`) while winning by **2.35x–3.70x wall-clock time** (`2.68s vs 6.30s` at `N=324`; `1.90s vs 7.03s` at `N=500`) and **7.0x–11.7x fewer CPU thread-seconds** (`10.7s vs 75.0s`; `7.6s vs 88.7s`). Do not re-introduce multi-seed worker races.

## 1. Objective & Pre-Registered Hypothesis
- **Objective:** Evaluate the wall-clock latency reduction achieved by running a 15-worker multiseed portfolio race in HiGHS vs. single deterministic MILP branching search across 324–500 candidate workouts.
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Multi-seed portfolio race yields no significant reduction in p95/p99 solve time or increases CPU contention overhead.
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):** Multi-seed race reduces p95 solve latency by $\ge 75\%$ by exploiting root heuristic seed variability.

## 2. Pre-Flight Ceteris-Paribus Hygiene Checklist
- [x] **Environment Quiescence:** Retested cleanly in `EXP-003` (2026-11-21) via `scripts/run_exp003_benchmark.py`.
- [x] **Cold vs. Warm State Control:** Separate worker processes per condition (`threads=2` per race worker vs `threads=4` single deterministic).
- [x] **Sample Size & Rigor:** $n = 20$ historical (contaminated); $n = 5$ paired isolated comparisons on $N \in \{324, 500\}$ in `EXP-003`.

## 3. Non-Goals & Safety Bounds
- **DO NOT** compromise weekly 80/20 zone constraints or TSS targets to achieve faster convergence.
- **DO NOT** allow unbounded worker process creation; strictly cap at 15 workers $\times$ 2 threads.

## 4. Control vs. Treatment Matrix & Test Corpus
- **Workload / Dataset:** Legacy 480-workout Excel workbook (2026-11-08); canonical/synthetic $N \in \{324, 500\}$ (`EXP-003` Condition 2, 2026-11-21).
- **Condition 1 (Control Baseline):** Single deterministic HiGHS solver (`threads=4` in `EXP-003`).
- **Condition 2 (Treatment A):** 15-seed parallel race (15 workers $\times$ 2 threads).

## 5. Telemetry Instruments & Sampling Commands
- Historical: `python -c "import main; main.run_highs_multiseed(df, num_workers=15)"` (retired).
- Authoritative retest: `python scripts/run_exp003_benchmark.py`.
