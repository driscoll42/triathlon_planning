# `EXP-002`: Closed-Loop 16-Week Season Simulation Under Real Profile and Perturbations

> **Status:** `QUEUED` (`QUEUED_SPEC`) — Pre-registered empirical validation for Phase 3 (`TASK-021`, `TASK-022`, `TASK-023`). Requires `TASK-021`..`023` physiology calibration to evaluate final ODI, Banister CTL, and TTE progression trajectories.

## 1. Objective & Pre-Registered Hypothesis
- **Objective:** Verify that the unified `WeekModel` MILP (`src/triplan/solver/engine/`, `ADR-017`) coupled with the 3-tier lexicographic constraint hierarchy (`ADR-013`), cross-week `WeekCarryIn` state (`ADR-019`), and 3-week rolling progression guard (`ADR-031`) produces a feasible, injury-resistant, non-degenerate 16-week race-block plan (plus 24-week re-entry block) for `CURRENT_ATHLETE`, including under acute mid-season perturbations and stochastic execution noise.
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Under mid-season perturbations or stochastic execution noise, at least one week degrades to an infeasible fallback, violates a Tier-0 orthopedic cap (rolling 48h TSS/ANS/strain, axial/neuromuscular, deadlift-run 24h lockout, single-run $\le 32\%$ when $\ge 4$ runs, in-model ACWR), or exhibits runaway ODI / ACWR drift.
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):** Across all 5 perturbation regimes, the closed-loop season planner completes with **0 fallback weeks**, **0 Tier-0 violations**, solved TSS within $\pm 10\%$ of effective target in $\ge 14/16$ race-block weeks, running mechanical ACWR $\le 1.20$ (re-entry) and $\le 1.25$ (post-re-entry) on every simulated day, single-run share $\le 32\%$ whenever $\ge 4$ runs are scheduled, and $\ge 20$ distinct workouts selected across the season.

## 2. Pre-Flight Ceteris-Paribus Hygiene Checklist
- [ ] **Environment Quiescence:** Execute on quiescent 32-thread workstation with single process-wide HiGHS thread setting (`src/triplan/solver/highs_runtime.py`).
- [ ] **Cold vs. Warm State Control:** Seed RNG deterministically (`np.random.default_rng(42)`) for stochastic execution noise; reset `AthletePhysiologicalState` and `WeekCarryIn` between conditions.
- [ ] **Sample Size & Rigor:** $n = 1$ deterministic trajectory for Conditions 1–4; $n = 20$ Monte Carlo trajectories for Condition 5 (report median, IQR, p95 ACWR, and worst-case week gap).

## 4. Control vs. Treatment Matrix & Test Corpus
- **Workload / Dataset:** `CURRENT_ATHLETE` (`config/athlete_profile.yaml`) + `build_default_candidate_library(include_8020=True)` (`data/workout_library.csv`) evaluated via `generate_closed_loop_season_schedule` (`src/triplan/control/season.py`) and `scripts/stress_test_season_perturbations.py`.
- **Condition 1 (Control — Nominal Season):** 16-week Olympic block (with and without 24-week re-entry block), zero external perturbations, 100% planned adherence.
- **Condition 2 (Treatment A — Week-5 Acute Illness):** `perturbations={5: {"type": "sickness"}}` ($-35\%$ target TSS, 0 heavy strength in Week 5), testing 3-week rolling progression guard (`ADR-031`) recovery in Weeks 6–7.
- **Condition 3 (Treatment B — Week-8 Pool Closure):** `perturbations={8: {"type": "pool_closure"}}` (`Swim` facility access all `False`, frequency `(0, 0)`), testing Bike/Run reallocation without ACWR spike.
- **Condition 4 (Treatment C — Week-11 Travel Compression):** `perturbations={11: {"type": "travel"}}` (`daily_time_caps = [60.0] * 7`), testing anchor step-down and Tier-1 relaxation reporting.
- **Condition 5 (Treatment D — Stochastic Execution Noise, $n=20$ seeds):** Each scheduled session completed with probability $p = 0.90$ and realized duration scaled by $U(0.80, 1.10)$, advancing `AthletePhysiologicalState` and `WeekCarryIn` from realized loads rather than planned loads.
