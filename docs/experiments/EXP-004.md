# `EXP-004`: Goal-Weight Sensitivity and Session-Structure Constraints Sweep

> **Status:** `QUEUED` (`QUEUED_SPEC`) — Pre-registered 81-point factorial sensitivity sweep validating that `ADR-020` session-structure constraints (grey-zone pre-filter $0 < q_i < q_{\min}$, $2 \le \text{key} \le 3$, hard 80/20 bounds) stabilize plan composition across a $\pm 4\times$ goal-weight range.

## 1. Objective & Pre-Registered Hypothesis
- **Objective:** Measure how sensitive weekly plan composition and 80/20 zone distribution are to the Tier-2 dimensionless soft-goal weights (`w_tss`, `w_zone`, `w_sport_dur`, `w_sport_tss` / `w_durability` in `src/triplan/solver/engine/builders/goals.py`), and determine whether the `ADR-020` session-structure constraints (`src/triplan/solver/engine/builders/session_structure.py` and `intensity_structure.py`) make weekly schedule quality invariant to weight tuning.
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Varying Tier-2 soft-goal weights across a $\times 0.25, \times 1.0, \times 4.0$ factorial grid causes weekly low-intensity time-in-zone ($Z_1 + Z_2$) to drift outside $[78\%, 86\%]$, admits grey-zone medium workouts ($0 < q_i < q_{\min}$), or alters selected workout sets by $> 2$ sessions near the neutral weight vector.
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):** With `ADR-020` session-structure constraints enabled (Treatment B), $0$ grey-zone workouts ($0 < q_i < q_{\min}$) are selected, low-intensity share remains in $[78\%, 85\%]$ across all 81 weight combinations at 300, 500, and 700 TSS, and Jaccard similarity against the neutral ($1\times, 1\times, 1\times, 1\times$) plan exceeds $0.70$ across the interior grid.

## 2. Pre-Flight Ceteris-Paribus Hygiene Checklist
- [ ] **Environment Quiescence:** Single deterministic HiGHS thread count per process; no concurrent heavy workloads.
- [ ] **Cold vs. Warm State Control:** Identical candidate pool (`build_default_candidate_library(include_8020=True)`) and `MesoCycleTargetsAndConstraints` baseline across all $81 \times 3 \times 2 = 486$ solves.
- [ ] **Sample Size & Rigor:** Full factorial design: $3^4 = 81$ weight vectors $\times$ $3$ TSS targets ($300, 500, 700$) $\times$ $2$ structural variants ($N = 486$ deterministic MILP solves).

## 4. Control vs. Treatment Matrix & Test Corpus
- **Workload / Dataset:** Default candidate pool (`build_default_candidate_library()`) solved across three weekly load levels (`target_weekly_tss` $\in \{300, 500, 700\}$) and $3^4 = 81$ multipliers ($\times 0.25, \times 1.0, \times 4.0$) on the 4 primary Tier-2 normalized goal weights (`w_tss`, `w_zone`, `w_sport_dur`, `w_sport_tss`).
- **Condition 1 (Control — Variant A: Unstructured Soft-Goal Model):** `EngineOptions` with `grey_zone_prefilter=False`, `key_session_bounds=False`, and `canonical_8020=False` (relying solely on Tier-2 zone/TSS soft goals).
- **Condition 2 (Treatment — Variant B: `ADR-020` Structured Model):** `EngineOptions` with `grey_zone_prefilter=True` ($0 < q_i < 5$ removed), `key_session_bounds=True` ($2 \le \sum \text{key}_i x_{i,d} \le 3$), `min_stride_sessions=2`, and `canonical_8020=True` ($Z_1+Z_2 \ge 78\%$, $Z_3+Z_Y+Z_4+Z_5 \le 20\%$).

## 5. Telemetry Instruments & Sampling Commands

- **Measurement Commands:**

## 6. Pre-Registered Go/No-Go Decision Rule
Lock the production Tier-2 default weight vector in `MesoCycleTargetsAndConstraints` (`src/triplan/model/candidates.py`) **ONLY IF** Variant B achieves 0 grey-zone workouts across all 243 Variant-B solves, 100% compliance with $Z_1+Z_2 \ge 78\%$ and $Z_3+Z_Y+Z_4+Z_5 \le 20\%$, and median normalized TSS deviation $\le 5\%$ on the Pareto frontier.
