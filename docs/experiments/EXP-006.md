# `EXP-006`: Load-Model Calibration Against Historical Garmin and Lifting Telemetry

> **Status:** `COMPLETE_ADOPTED` (`TIER_1_ISOLATED`) — Executed 2026-11-21 via `python scripts/exp006_load_calibration.py` (`data/experiments/EXP-006/fitted_parameters.json`) and `pytest tests/test_exp006_calibration.py`. Informs `TASK-021` (`ADR-034`), `TASK-025` (unified morning readiness $\alpha_d$), and `TASK-058` (walk-jog impact load prior).

## 1. Objective & Pre-Registered Hypothesis
- **Objective:** Calibrate the physiological load and readiness parameters in `src/triplan/physiology/` and `src/triplan/control/mpc.py` against the athlete's local historical telemetry (`data/garmin/activities/activities_summary.json` with 2,020 activities / 680 with power, `data/garmin/wellness/hrv_summaries.json` with 270 nights of rMSSD, `daily_summaries.json`, `sleep_summaries.json`, and `data/lifting/lifting_summary.json`):
  1. **Banister PMC Decay:** Compare current `ALPHA_CTL = 2/(42+1)` and `ALPHA_ATL = 2/(7+1)` (`src/triplan/physiology/state.py:14-15`) against exact exponential decay $\alpha = 1 - e^{-1/\tau}$ ($\tau_{\text{CTL}}=42\text{d}, \tau_{\text{ATL}}=7\text{d}$).
  2. **Planned vs. Realized Zone Midpoints:** Compare realized easy-session Intensity Factors ($IF$) against the library's assumed zone midpoints (`RUN_ZONE_IF = (0.63, 0.82, 0.97, 1.115, 1.35)`, `BIKE_ZONE_IF = (0.60, 0.765, 0.955, 1.06, 1.30)` in `src/triplan/library/build.py:48-49`).
  3. **Running Mechanical Strain Exponent ($\beta$):** Evaluate daily running strain $S^{\text{run}}_i = \sum_z t_{i,z} p_z^\beta$ across $\beta \in \{1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0\}$ and its induced 7d/28d EWMA ACWR distribution across historical running blocks.
  4. **Morning Readiness ($\alpha_d$) Z-Score Mapping:** Fix the RHR SD bug (`src/triplan/control/mpc.py:134` dividing RHR deviation by `rmssd_baseline_std * 0.5`) using separate 28-day rolling means and standard deviations for nocturnal rMSSD and RHR, and calibrate $(a, b, c)$ in $\alpha_d = \text{clip}(1 + a z_{\text{HRV}} - b z_{\text{RHR}} + c z_{\text{sleep}}, 0.60, 1.00)$.
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Current asserted constants match historical telemetry within $\le 5\%$, and fixing Banister decay or RHR SD changes CTL or $\alpha_d$ distributions negligibly.
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):**
  - (a) Current `2/(N+1)` PMC decay overstates 6-week constant-load CTL by $> 25\%$ relative to exact $\alpha = 1 - e^{-1/\tau}$ ($86.5$ vs $63.2$ after $42\text{d}$).
  - (b) Library Z2 midpoint ($0.82\,CP \implies 67\text{ TSS/h}$) overstates realized easy-run $IF$ by $> 10\%$.
  - (c) Using separate 28-day rolling rMSSD SD and RHR SD with calibrated weights $(a, b, c)$ maps $\alpha_d \in [0.60, 1.00]$ such that $10\%\text{--}15\%$ of historical days fall into the Caution/Red zone ($\alpha_d < 0.85$).

## 8. Architectural Conclusion & Action Taken
- **Key Findings:**
  1. **Banister PMC:** Exact $\alpha = 1 - e^{-1/\tau}$ (`banister_alpha` in `src/triplan/physiology/load_model.py`) matches the closed-form 42d/7d step responses to `1.2e-13%` / `2.2e-14%` (Go/No-Go #1 PASS), eliminating the `+36.8%` CTL and `+37.1%` ATL step-response overstatement of `2/(N+1)`.
  2. **Zone Midpoints:** Realized Bike easy-session $IF$ (`0.753`, 95% CI `[0.744, 0.762]`) matches `BIKE_MID7[1] = 0.765` within `1.65%`. Historical Run sessions cannot identify `RUN_MID7[1]` against the Nov 2026 re-entry `run_cp = 200W` because earlier training blocks were run at higher historical CP (~235–245W steady in 2025).
  3. **Run Strain Exponent $\beta$:** Historical running ACWR distributions are nearly invariant across $\beta \in \{1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0\}$ (`56.4%–56.6% > 1.25`), while Z4/Z2 strain weighting scales from `1.36x` to `2.51x`. Without injury-onset timestamps, $\beta = 3.0$ is explicitly retained as a sensitivity-bounded Tier-0 prior (Go/No-Go #3 PASS). Across `419` historical lifting sessions, `5` same-day and `23` next-day lift-run overlaps occurred (median axial load `42.1`).
  4. **Morning Readiness $\alpha_d$:** The `TASK-025` formula with separate 28-day rolling RHR SD, `(0.08, 0.05, 0.04)` weights, and `[0.60, 1.00]` bounds places `14.45%` of historical nights below `0.85` and `6.46%` below `0.75` (Go/No-Go #2 PASS).
